# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
publishing another person's private information, or other unprofessional
conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not
align with this Code of Conduct. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the
project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
